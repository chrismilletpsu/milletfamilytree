# Sources

OCR text of volumes searched for the tree. The `.txt` files are git-ignored; each is listed here
with its archive.org identifier so it can be fetched again
(`https://archive.org/download/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`).

- `historyoffayette00elli` — Franklin Ellis, *History of Fayette County, Pennsylvania* (1882).
  Clemmer: Springhill officers (Jasper Clemmer, school director 1846; L. B. Clemmer, 1880); the
  Smithfield Clemmers (Dr. Upton L., b. 1816 Allegany Co., Md.; Gideon G.; Lewis).
- `nelsonsbiographi01shep` — *Nelson's Biographical Dictionary … of Fayette County* (1900).
- `genealogicalpersfc02jord` — Jordan, *Genealogical and Personal History of Fayette County*
  (1912), vol. 2 (vols. 1 and 3 have no text layer on archive.org).
- `historyofpreston00wile` — S. T. Wiley, *History of Preston County (West Virginia)* (1882).
  Roster of Co. C, 3rd W.Va. Infantry: Henry H. Wilkins; J. H. Blaney.
- `historyofpreston01mort`, `historyofpreston02mort_0` — Oren F. Morton, *A History of Preston
  County, West Virginia* (1914). Jonathan Blaney, from Monongalia about 1818, north of Masontown.

Searched in all of them for: Clemmer, Rumble, Varner, Dunham, Dewalt, Halphin/Halfin, Wilkins,
Blaney. No mention of Andrew Clemmer, Godfrey Rumble, Martin or John Dunham by name.
