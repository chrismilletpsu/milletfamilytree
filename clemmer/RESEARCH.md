# Clemmer research log

What was searched, what was found, and what was not. Every negative records the exact source and
the years and places it covers, so a search isn't repeated and a wall can be told from a gap in
coverage.

## 26 Sep 2026 — first pass

### Found

- **William Bryan Clemmer (1908–1982).** The 1940 WWII draft card (Point Marion; born 27 June 1908,
  Fayette Co.; contact his wife, Margaret Belle Clemmer); censuses for 1910, 1920, 1940 and 1950; the
  SSDI (died May 1982, Point Marion); Find a Grave memorial 144874872 (Evergreen Memorial Park, Point
  Marion). FamilySearch's copy of the Find a Grave index puts the grave in Zellwood, Florida. That is
  a place-standardisation error: the memorial itself says Point Marion.
- **The marriage.** Monongalia Co., W.Va., license pp. 432–433, read from the image at WV Archives
  (saved in `assets/`). Married at Morgantown on 14 Oct 1938. It names both sets of parents:
  **Amadee Clemmer and Elizabeth Wilkins**, and **Martin Dunham and Emma Miller**. It gives
  Margaret's birth as 22 Mar 1916 at Smithfield; the grave index says 21 March.
- **Margaret's family.** The 1930 census, Georges Twp. (which contains Smithfield), has Martin
  Dunham, 66, Emma, 49, and Margaret B. among nine children. The license's parents and birthplace
  make the match; it is not carried as probable.
- **Thomas / Neil.** The 1940 census's "Thomas Clemmer, 6" is Margaret's son Thomas Neil Dunham
  (1935–2021). He is Neil T. Dunham, 14, in 1950, and Find a Grave links him to William. His 2021
  obituary names his mother, William, and Bernard, William, Phyllis and Jacqueline. **Not on the
  page yet**: he was born before the marriage and died recently, so whether and how to show him is
  the family's call.
- **Amadee H. Clemmer (1880–1921).** His PA death certificate, 49787, is saved in
  `assets/ancestry/`. It names his parents as **Lebius Clemmer and Caroline Rumble**. The date of
  death conflicts: the certificate says 4 May 1921, Find a Grave 11 Apr 1921. The certificate is
  preferred.
- **The 1880 census, Springhill Twp.** Lebous Clemmer, 49, Caroline, 39, and children Jasper, Elle
  M., Oney A., Joseph, Oliver S. and Amada (3 months). This independently confirms the certificate's
  parents.
- **Lebbeus Bigelow Clemmer (1832–1909).** His PA death certificate, 15443, is saved. He was a
  brick molder, **son of Andrew Clemmer (b. Pa.) and Moriah Halphin (b. W.Va.)**. The informant was
  Frank Clemmer of Cheat Haven, probably a son born after 1880.

### Conflicts and corrections

- Find a Grave memorial 39473331 links **Iona Alice Clemmer Molesy (1868–1954)** as Amadee's
  mother. The 1880 census shows her (as Oney A., 10) as his sister, and his death certificate names
  Caroline Rumble. Treat that Find a Grave family link as wrong.

### Leads not yet followed

- Jasper Clemmer, 16 Jan 1840 – 6 Jul 1922, Springhill. His PA death certificate index gives
  parents Andrew and Mariah, so he is probably Lebbeus's brother. Read the certificate.
- Jasper E. Clemmer, 29 Nov 1862 – 26 Dec 1931, Springhill (Ancestry PA death certificate index;
  parents "Lebby" and Caroline). He is Amadee's brother.
- Lebbeus in the 1850 census (an Ancestry hint), a Civil War draft registration ("Lebbeus B
  Clemmer"), and a PA marriage (1852–1968 index).
- Amadee: a PA marriage index entry ("Amedee Clemmer"), and a WWI draft card indexed as "Amedie Gans
  Clemmer" — the middle name is still to be checked against the image.
- Martin Dunham (b. c. 1864) and Emma Miller (b. c. 1881): their death certificates and earlier
  censuses.

### Negatives

- Ancestry, *Pennsylvania Death Certificates 1906–1972*: no Elizabeth Clemmer dying in 1931 in the
  index. No Elizabeth born c. 1884 died 1931 with father surname Wilkins either (not an exhaustive
  check; she may be indexed under a mangled name).
- Find a Grave raises a Cloudflare "verify you are human" check on most memorial pages. Chris
  cleared it for memorial 144874872; other memorials were read through the indexes instead.

## 26 Sep 2026 — second pass, both sides

### Found

- **Andrew Clemmer's household, 1850**, Springhill Twp., dwelling 235: Andrew, 40, Maria, 38,
  and nine children, including Lebbens, 18, and "Gasper", 9, who is the Jasper (1840–1922) whose
  death certificate names Andrew and Mariah as his parents. In 1880 Andrew, 69, is a laborer in
  Nicholas Ganow's household in Springhill. FamilySearch's shared tree calls him "Andrew Blosser
  Clemmer, 1810–1887" (tree person 2S4K-ZSG). That is a lead only: nothing read here yet gives
  the middle name or the death date.
- **Caroline Rumble's parents.** Her PA death certificate, 114721 (d. 24 Dec 1909), names
  Godfrey Rumble and Rebecca Varner; the informant was her son Jasper Clemmer Sr. of Cheat Haven.
- **Elizabeth Wilkins's parents.** Fayette Co. marriage license docket 44, p. 47, license 19339,
  4 Sep 1907: Eliza Wilkins, 24, born Morgantown, W.Va., daughter of Harry and Mary. The 1900
  census, Springhill Twp., has Eliza E. Wilkens, born Sept 1884 in W.Va., with Henry H. (b. Dec
  1843, W.Va.) and Mary C. (b. July 1844, W.Va.). The name, month, year, state and township all
  agree, and Harry is the usual nickname for Henry, so the join is carried as proven. The same
  license names Amadee's parents as "Leebbius and Caroline Clemmer": a third independent source.
- **Martin Dunham's parents.** His PA death certificate, 72324 (d. 26 Aug 1943), names John Dunham
  and Rebecca Dewalt, both born in Fayette Co.; his 1899 license names John and Rebecca.
- **Emma Miller's parents.** Her PA death certificate, 26280 (d. 15 Mar 1942), names Wm Miller and
  "Ida Emme" (the index reads "Ian Emme"); the 1899 license names Wm. G. and Ida.

### Negatives

- Elizabeth (Wilkins) Clemmer's death, 2 Apr 1931 by her grave. Ancestry PA death certificates:
  every woman indexed as dying on 2 Apr 1931 (168, all counties) was read; none died in Fayette Co.
  at her age. West Virginia Vital Research Records deaths: no *Clemmer* in any county in 1931.
  Monongalia Clemmer deaths do show up for other years, so the search works. Next: try 1930–1932,
  and her name spelled Clemer or Klemmer.
